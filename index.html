Selected Works

Heterogeneous Collaborative Pursuit via Coverage Control Driven by Fokker-Planck Equations [PDF]
R. Lin, S. Kim, M. Egerstedt — IEEE Transactions on Robotics (T-RO), 2025. DOI: 10.1109/TRO.2025.3559420

An ecologically inspired multi-agent collaboration framework is developed for heterogeneous robots to enhance their capabilities through collaboration, enabling them to accomplish tasks that would otherwise be beyond their reach.

Disentangled Control of Multi-Agent Systems [PDF]
R. Lin, G. Notomista, M. Egerstedt — Accepted to IEEE Transactions on Control of Network Systems (TCNS), 2026.

This paper develops a general framework for decentralized multi-agent control, which also solves a long-standing open problem: Decentralized coverage control for time-varying density functions.

“Hierarchy of Needs” for Robots: Control Synthesis for Compositions of Hierarchical, Complex Objectives [PDF]
R. Lin, M. Egerstedt — IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA), 2025. DOI: 10.1109/ICRA55743.2025.11128842

Inspired by Maslow's hierarchy of needs, this paper develops an integrated planning and control framework enabling robots to pursue hierarchical, complex objectives in real time, acting only when driven by unmet needs.
